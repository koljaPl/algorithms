#### A genuinely interesting problem

This is one of the few problems that I consider genuinely non-trivial and, in its own way, complex, as it is impossible to memorise a pattern for such problems due to their ‘peculiarities’, meaning that they occur only once in several thousand problems.

This problem cannot be solved in slow languages such as Python, even with the right idea and additional optimisations, and even in C++, the threshold is difficult to clear.

The specific nature of the problem plays a role here, and because of this, the problem requires some thought.

Unless you delve quite deeply into the subject of algorithms, it is impossible to guess the solution, and at the IOI I present precisely such problems, as they require not only the right approach but also a deep understanding of the entire subject in principle.
