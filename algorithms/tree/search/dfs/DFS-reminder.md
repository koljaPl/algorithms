#### What is the difference between in-order, post-order and pre-order DFS?

The difference is simple: when you add an element to your answer, if it’s inorder, it will be inserted 
between the entry into the first element (left or right) and the entry into the second element. Postorder, 
obviously, comes after all entries, and preorder comes before. It’s all quite clear; I think preorder is 
used most often, but I could be wrong.
